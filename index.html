The Artist Behind the Work

Sabrina Luna — painter, designer, dreamer

Luna & Co. began the way all great studios do — with an obsession, a little courage, and a whole lot of paint. Three years ago, Sabrina Luna turned her apartment living room into a studio and a passion into a livelihood.

Today, Luna & Co. is a full-service creative studio specializing in hand-painted wall art, murals, graphic design, and licensed illustration. Sabrina's work has been featured in pop-up galleries, local coffee shops, and across thousands of home décor feeds.

Her philosophy is simple: art should be accessible, alive in your everyday space, and made with enough intention that it grows more meaningful over time.

Three years.
Thousands of walls.
